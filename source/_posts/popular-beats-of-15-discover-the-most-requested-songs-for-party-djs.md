## Popular Beats of '15: Discover the Most-Requested Songs for Party DJs!

[![Most requested songs from DJs in 2015](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/top200requestedsongs2015-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/top200requestedsongs2015-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "Top 200 requested songs of 2015")

The proverbial door has hit 2015 square in the tuches and the tally is complete — here’s the list of the **top 200 requested songs from DJs in 2015**.

Each year [DJ Intelligence](http://www.djintelligence.com/) compiles the **top requested songs** of the previous year after **analyzing millions of client requests** made through their music request system, largely from weddings and other private events. DJ Intelligence claims **you will not find a more accurate representation of what songs are actually being requested** from mobile DJs world-wide.

![Mobile DJ using DEX 3 RE](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/06/redmobile3-mobiledj.jpg?fit=207%2C300&ssl=1 "Mobile DJ using DEX 3 RE")

Millions of requests is certainly a size-able pile of data, so we trust that their chart is accurate for most DJs – other than those playing in niche markets.

A quick search in [Pulselocker](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) (in-app streaming service that’s now baked into both of our **DJ mixing software** platforms) yielded most of these tracks, other than those under Universal Music Group, who’s content won’t yet be ingested into the system until late this month.

Personally, I always get a kick out of these charts. I like to **dig through my history list in DEX 3** to see how the top 200 requested songs charts match up with my requests over the year. While I don’t do many private functions anymore, it seems most of the tracks in the list I’ve played at least a handful of times throughout 2015.
