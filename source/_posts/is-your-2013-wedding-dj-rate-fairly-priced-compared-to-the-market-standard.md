**_What's your Mobile DJ rate for weddings?_**  

A recent blog article published by [**_The Knot_**](http://blog.theknot.com/2014/03/27/average-wedding-cost-2014/)regarding the average cost of a wedding in 2013 suggests the average DJs rate was **_$1,083_**. That's a U.S. nationwide average, so it's safe to assume many Mobile DJs charge significantly more. 

**_If you're not already charging at least $1,083 for your Wedding DJ package, why not?_**
