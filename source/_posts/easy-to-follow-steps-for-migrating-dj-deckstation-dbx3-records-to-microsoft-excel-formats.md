Since our [DEX 3 DJ program](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) is not only designed to mix music and music video, but to also host karaoke, we’ve been asked many times how to create a songbook from DEX 3’s database file. Well here you go, _and it’s a piece of cake._

In this quick-tip video demonstration we show you how to open up the DEX 3 database file into Microsoft Excel 2013 (_other spread sheet applications, including the free app that comes with the [Open Office suite from Apache](https://www.openoffice.org/), should work well. Although I can’t vouch if it’s as easy as it is in our video demonstration regarding automatic formatting_).
